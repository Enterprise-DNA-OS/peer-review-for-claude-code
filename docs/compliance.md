# Editorial evidence checks

Checked 8 November 2026. These checks inspect recorded evidence and dates. They do not determine scientific merit, certify compliance or replace an editor's judgement.

| Rule | Check | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| CONFLICT | Accepted or completed current-round invitation has unknown or declared competing interests | COPE ethical guidelines ask reviewers to disclose competing interests. Editors must resolve them before the CLI accepts a review. |
| CONFIDENTIAL | Accepted or completed current-round invitation lacks acknowledgement date | COPE and the US Office of Research Integrity require confidentiality. A recorded date is evidence of an acknowledgement, not proof of conduct. |
| OVERDUE | Active current-round invitation passed its deadline | COPE asks reviewers to agree a timescale and report delays. The actual deadline is journal policy, not legislation. |
| EDITOR | Active manuscript has no named editor | Local accountability policy. |
| EVIDENCE | Completed invitation has no received review record | Local evidence policy. |
| DECISION | Accepted or rejected manuscript lacks a matching current-round decision | Local decision-trace policy, especially for imported history. |
| RETENTION | Manuscript retention review date has passed | NZ Privacy Act principle 9: keep personal information no longer than required for its lawful purpose. Review necessity, do not automatically erase research records. |

Sources: [COPE ethical guidelines for peer reviewers](https://members.publicationethics.org/sites/default/files/cope-ethical-guidelines-peer-reviewers-v2_0.pdf), [ORI confidentiality](https://ori.hhs.gov/content/chapter-10-peer-review-Preserving-confidentiality), [NZ Privacy Commissioner principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/).

The default journal target is two eligible reviews. This is editable journal policy, not a universal publishing rule. Only current-round completed reviews with a cleared interest declaration and confidentiality acknowledgement count. Conflicting recommendations are shown to the editor, never resolved automatically. A desk rejection is available only before external review. Earlier rounds remain visible and never satisfy a later round's target.

Real manuscript content and reviewer reports stay in your approved document store. Keep only necessary metadata and references here. An operator must not upload confidential research to a third-party model without the journal's permission and suitable terms. HTML exports are internal documents. The decision-letter template omits reviewer identities, but a person must still check the editor's free-text rationale before sending.

RLS is enabled and PUBLIC privileges revoked on records and views. This is an administrator-operated package. A shared installation needs least-privilege database roles, journal-level separation, access reviews, backups and retention policy. Activity records are not tamper-proof against a database administrator.
